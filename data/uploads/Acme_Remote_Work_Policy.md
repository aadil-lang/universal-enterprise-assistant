# Acme Corporation — Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** POL-REM-2026-01  
**Category:** Human Resources & IT Security  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Status:** Active Governing Policy  

---

## 1. Overview and Purpose
Acme Corporation supports flexible remote work arrangements for eligible employees. This policy outlines the standards, equipment provisions, and security requirements necessary to maintain productivity and protect corporate assets while working remotely.

---

## 2. Eligibility and Approval
Remote work eligibility is determined by department directors based on job responsibilities and performance standards. Employees must maintain reliable high-speed internet connectivity and a dedicated, quiet workspace.

---

## 3. Home-Office Ergonomic Stipend
To support ergonomic and productive home work environments:
- **Stipend Amount:** Eligible full-time remote and hybrid employees receive a **one-time home-office ergonomic stipend of $300**.
- **Eligible Purchases:** The stipend may be applied toward ergonomic office chairs, standing desks, external keyboards, mice, or monitor risers.
- **Expense Reimbursement:** Receipts must be submitted through the corporate expense reporting portal within 60 days of purchase.

---

## 4. IT Security and VPN Requirements
To safeguard company data, intellectual property, and internal systems:
- **Mandatory Daily VPN Connection:** Employees accessing corporate applications, internal networks, or cloud databases must connect via the corporate Acme VPN at the start of each working day.
- **Network Security:** Connecting to unencrypted public Wi-Fi networks (such as in airports, cafes, or hotels) without the Acme VPN enabled is strictly prohibited.
- **Device Usage:** Only company-issued hardware may be used to access internal code repositories, customer databases, and production environments.
- **Reporting Security Incidents:** Any suspected credential compromise, device theft, or unauthorized access attempt must be reported immediately to the IT Helpdesk via an emergency ticket.
